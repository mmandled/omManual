SAS Bulletin — Online Managing Editor Manual

An internal knowledge base and training manual created for the Online Management (OM) Team of SAS Bulletin for Academic Year 2026–2027.

This manual was created to provide OMs with a centralized reference for the team's workflow, production management, social media operations, and other responsibilities.

Purpose

The SAS Bulletin OM Manual serves as a practical guide for Online Managers, covering:

OM responsibilities
Production workflow
ClickUp task management
Production updates and coordination
Meta Business Suite
Facebook and Instagram management
Content scheduling and publishing
Community management
Task designation
Monthly analytics
Troubleshooting
OM quick references

The goal is to make the OM workflow easier to understand, follow, and maintain throughout the academic year.
